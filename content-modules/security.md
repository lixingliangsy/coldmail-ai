# ColdMail AI — Security & Compliance

## What we handle (email data)
ColdMail AI processes the **prospect details, company research, and draft copy you submit** to write cold outreach. These inputs may contain personal data (names, roles, companies) and the generated emails are outbound messages to real recipients. We do not train public models on your submissions without explicit consent.

- We process submitted text only to generate your cold email.
- We do not auto-send; you review and connect your own sender/ESP and confirm before send.
- We screen for CAN-SPAM / GDPR (Art.6 lawful basis, unsubscribe) expectations but do not guarantee deliverability or compliance. ref: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- We minimize retained data and avoid storing credentials (ESP API keys, tokens) longer than needed.

## Data handling commitments
- Input is used only to generate your result.
- We minimize retained data and avoid storing secrets longer than needed.
- Transfers use standard encryption in transit.

## Compliance posture
We keep data handling simple and minimal by design.

> **Honesty note:** ColdMail AI is a tool that helps you prepare and assess. It does **not** guarantee compliance, 100% coverage, or that you will never miss a requirement. Treat its output as decision-support, not legal sign-off.

## Subprocessors
We rely on our hosting provider (Vercel) and payment processor (Waffo). No data is sold to third parties.

References: ref: OWASP Top 10; GDPR Art.5 (data minimization)
